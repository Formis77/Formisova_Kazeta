# Kazeta – offline music player

Plays music files you own, straight from your phone, with no internet.

## Put it on your iPhone (about 5 minutes, free)

1. Make a free account at github.com (in Safari is fine).
2. Tap **+** → **New repository**. Name it `kazeta`, set it to **Public**, tick **Add a README**, tap **Create repository**.
3. Tap **Add file** → **Upload files** and upload everything from this zip
   (index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest and the three .png icons). Tap **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings** → **Pages**. Under *Branch* pick **main** and **/(root)**, tap **Save**.
5. Wait a minute, then open `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/kazeta/` in **Safari**.
6. Tap the **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
7. Open Kazeta from the homescreen icon (not from Safari) and import your songs there.
   Songs imported in Safari and in the homescreen app are stored separately.

## Adding songs
Save MP3 / M4A / FLAC / WAV files to the **Files** app first (AirDrop from a computer, or download them),
then in Kazeta tap **Import** and select them. Title, artist, album and cover art are read from the files' tags.
Files named `Artist - Title.mp3` are also understood.

## Good to know
- After the first open the app works in flight mode.
- In *Your Library* tap **Keep songs on this phone** so iOS doesn't clear them.
- Keep your original files in the Files app as a backup. iOS can clear web-app storage if you don't open the app for many weeks.
- Lock screen and headphone buttons control playback.
